About BWS

A wholly owned Bumiputera contractor, built by people from the industry

BWS Engineering Sdn. Bhd. was formed in October 2002 by a group of Bumiputera with many years of experience in the oil and gas industry, particularly in construction and maintenance services.

The company is run by its shareholders, who sit on the Board of Directors and in Management, and employs qualified professionals with thorough experience across engineering and construction disciplines.

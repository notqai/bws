About BWS

A wholly owned Bumiputera engineering contractor

Founded in October 2002 by Bumiputera professionals from the oil and gas industry, BWS has grown from minor plant maintenance in Miri into a Grade G7 contractor delivering complete EPCC packages.

Supported by its own facilities in Miri and Bintulu, the company delivers engineering, construction and maintenance services to the oil and gas, power and public sectors across Malaysia.

Core services

  • Industrial Plant Construction and Maintenance: Construction and maintenance for oil and gas facilities, power generation plants and wood-based factories.
  • Mechanical and Piping Work: Equipment installation and testing, piping fabrication, installation and testing, plus blasting and painting.
  • Civil, Building and Structural Steel Construction: From site clearing and earthwork to foundations, industrial buildings, warehouses and jetties.
  • Electrical and Instrumentation Work: Cable laying, equipment installation, instrument calibration and testing, and commissioning assistance.

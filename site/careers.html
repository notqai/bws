Build your career with BWS

Join a Sarawak contractor that has been building and maintaining oil and gas facilities since 2002.

Working at BWS

A career in engineering and construction

Our workforce comprises engineers, supervisors and skilled tradespeople in civil, mechanical, piping, and electrical and instrumentation disciplines, based at our Miri head office and Bintulu fabrication yard.

Our work spans industrial plant construction and maintenance, mechanical and piping, civil and structural steel, and electrical and instrumentation, for some of the region’s major oil and gas operators and EPC contractors.

Safety-first culture

A safety-first workplace

Safety is the first responsibility of every employee. All personnel are inducted, trained and supervised in line with our HSE policy and ISO 45001:2018 management system.

Build your career with BWS

Join a Sarawak contractor that has been building and maintaining oil and gas facilities since 2002.

Working at BWS

Working with experienced people

BWS employs qualified professionals with thorough experience in various engineering and construction disciplines, working from our main office and workshop in Miri and our fabrication yard in Bintulu.

Our work spans industrial plant construction and maintenance, mechanical and piping, civil and structural steel, and electrical and instrumentation, for some of the region’s major oil and gas operators and EPC contractors.

Safety-first culture

Everyone goes home safe

We conduct our business only after taking account of the health and safety of our employees. No job shall start or continue without due regard to Safety, Health and Environment, and every member of the team is expected to uphold that.

Featured projects

A closer look at three projects: what we were asked to do, and the results our clients recorded.

Malaysia LNG · 2022 – 2025 · Bintulu, Sarawak

EPCC of the Power Import Project for MLNG

A joint venture between Transgrid Ventures Sdn Bhd and BWS Engineering, delivering new power import facilities at the PETRONAS LNG Complex from design through to handover.

Scope

  • Management, administration and detailed design engineering
  • Site preparation, earthworks, procurement, storage and preservation
  • Construction, equipment installation, testing and pre-commissioning
  • Tie-ins and modifications of piping and cables to the existing facilities
  • Commissioning, start-up and handover

Results

  • MSOSH Gold Class 1 Award for very good OSH performance in 2023

Rural Water Supply Department (JBALB) Sarawak · 2019 – 2025 · Miri, Sarawak

Miri Water Supply Source Development Phase 2

Part of the Sarawak Water Grid Programme: transferring raw water from Batang Baram to Sungai Bakong and Sungai Liku, to meet the demand of Miri city and its surroundings up to 2030.

Scope

  • Earthworks and infrastructure, including the service road
  • 1,600 mm diameter raw water pipeline, including twin 1.2 m submarine pipes
  • Pumping station, guard house and genset house
  • High-tension line and mechanical and electrical works

PETRONAS Dagangan · 2014 – 2015 · Miri, Sarawak

EPCC of the PDB Miri Fuel Terminal

Engineering, procurement, construction and commissioning of new facilities at the PETRONAS Dagangan fuel terminal in Miri, with a team of about 60.

Scope

  • Administration building, MSB room and substation, and other civil and structural work
  • Fabrication and erection of tanks and accessories
  • Firefighting facilities and other mechanical and electrical work

Results

  • 150,000 man-hours without lost-time injury (PETRONAS Dagangan certificates at 30,000 and 150,000 man-hours, 2015)

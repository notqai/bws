Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal data, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

This notice explains what personal data BWS Engineering Sdn Bhd collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have. It is published in English and Bahasa Melayu.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

BWS Engineering Sdn Bhd (597192-V), Lot 1743, Block 3 MCLD, Piasau Industrial Estate Phase III, Jalan Prunus 1, 98000 Miri, Sarawak, Malaysia, is responsible for the personal data described in this notice.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, company, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • Job applications: your name, email address, phone number, location, years of experience, the position you are applying for, your message, and your CV with anything it contains, such as qualifications, work history and certificates.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our web host keeps short-term server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) to run the site securely. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. If you do not provide them, we cannot reply to your enquiry or consider your application. Everything else is optional.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotations.
  • To assess your job application, contact you about it and, if you are hired, start your employment records.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse of its forms.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for marketing.

Who we share it with

  • BWS staff who need it to handle your enquiry or application.
  • Service providers that run our website, email and application system for us, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Government or regulatory authorities, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers, such as our email services, may store data on servers outside Malaysia. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data is protected to a standard comparable to Malaysian law.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to deal with them and for our business records.
  • Job applications and CVs are kept for up to 12 months after they are received and then deleted, unless you join BWS. You can ask us to delete yours sooner.

How we protect it

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). CVs and enquiries are kept in BWS’s own email and records, and only the staff who handle them can see them.

Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • give you access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • stop or limit using your data, or withdraw your consent (we may then be unable to continue with your enquiry or application);
  • delete your job application and CV.

Email bws@bwsengineeringsb.com or write to our Miri office. We will reply within 21 days.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Health, safety, environment and quality

Our policies, certifications and recognition in health, safety, environment and quality.

HSE policy

Our HSE policy

Health · Safety · Environment

BWS Engineering Sdn Bhd is committed to conducting its operations with full regard to Health, Safety and Environmental (HSE) requirements. No work shall commence or continue without ensuring a safe and healthy workplace and protection of the environment.

To achieve this, the company undertakes to:

  • Create a safe and healthy workplace
  • Prevent all occupational diseases and accidents
  • Create an environment that recognises safe and healthy practice
  • Work in a manner that is friendly to the environment
  • Provide experienced, trained and competent management and supervisors

Our performance in Health, Safety and Environment is the ultimate yardstick of our success in running our business.

Quality policy

Our quality policy

BWS Engineering Sdn Bhd is committed to providing products and services of the highest quality to achieve total customer satisfaction through continually improving the effectiveness of its Quality Management System.

Our quality objectives:

  • Continuously improve our products and services by upgrading our people’s skills, with at least 4 hours of training for every employee each year
  • Provide and maintain high-quality services in compliance with ISO 9001, aiming for zero customer complaints
  • Assess vendors and subcontractors at least once a year
